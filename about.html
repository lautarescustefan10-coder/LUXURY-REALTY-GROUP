OUR STORY

Building Trust Since 2014

Founded in Montreal, Luxury Realty Group began with a simple vision: to redefine the rental experience for discerning clients who demand more than just four walls and a roof.

Over the past 12 years, we've grown from a boutique agency into one of Montreal's most trusted names in luxury real estate. Our portfolio spans the city's most prestigious neighbourhoods — from the tree-lined streets of Westmount to the vibrant energy of Downtown.

We believe that finding the right home is about more than matching specifications — it's about understanding lifestyles, anticipating needs, and delivering experiences that exceed expectations.
