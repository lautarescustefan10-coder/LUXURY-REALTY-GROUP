About THE SETAI ST MAARTEN

Experience unparalleled luxury at THE SETAI ST MAARTEN, where Caribbean elegance meets world-class amenities. This exclusive beachfront development offers sophisticated living spaces with breathtaking ocean views.

Nestled on the pristine shores of Saint Martin, THE SETAI offers residents access to private beaches, resort-style amenities, and the renowned Setai hospitality. Each residence features high-end finishes, spacious layouts, and panoramic views of the turquoise Caribbean Sea.
